RSA Calculator

Based on the RSA worksheet by JL Popyack (Drexel). Uses BigInt, so large values work, but factoring is by trial division: keep K below about 15 digits.

Step 1. Compute N as the product of two primes p and q

Step 2. Find a number equal to 1 mod r which can be factored
Step 3. Choose e and d with e·d ≡ 1 (mod r)

Split the factors of K into two numbers e and d, then check.


Step 4. Encode and decode messages

Enter a message in numeric form (e.g. ASCII codes). Each chunk must be smaller than N.


Diffie-Hellman Key Exchange

Agree on a shared secret over a public channel

Alice and Bob publicly agree on a prime P and a base G. Each keeps a private number, and they swap only the public values.



One person only: use just P, G and your private number (Alice's a), plus the public value you received.
